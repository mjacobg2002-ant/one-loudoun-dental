Meet your dentist

Dr. Dan Ashouripour, D.D.S.

General Dentist — Implant & Cosmetic Dentistry

Dr. Dan Ashouripour is a graduate of the Medical College of Virginia and has practiced dentistry for over 20 years, with extensive post-graduate training in implant dentistry as well as cosmetic, restorative, and surgical treatments.

His approach starts with patiently listening to each patient, followed by a thorough exam and an honest conversation about diagnosis and treatment options — no pressure, just a clear plan. His practice team speaks English, Spanish, French, Farsi, and Turkish to serve Ashburn's diverse community.
